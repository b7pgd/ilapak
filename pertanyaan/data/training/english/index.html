JACK Speaking Framework (Spontaneous)
J
Judgment / Position Mandatory Core objective: clear delivery of your primary stance, opinion, or answer.
A
Argument / Reason Mandatory * Logical flow support: explain why you hold this viewpoint (*crucial for opinions).
C
Concrete Example Optional Rhetorical asset: evidence, storytelling, or real-life illustrations if required.
K
Conclusion / Closing Optional Rhetorical closer: final emphasis (summary, recommendation, implication, or expectation).
🔥 Structural Ideation Priorities

1. Dissect Thoughts (Priority #1)

Avoid blending multiple ideas into long, complex paragraphs. Construct a linear sequence in your mind beforehand: Idea 1 → Idea 2 → Idea 3 → Conclusion. Rule: 1 Idea = 1 Sentence!

🔗 English Discourse Connectors (Priority #2)
A. Adding Ideas
Furthermore, Besides, In addition, Moreover, What's more
B. Reason / Cause
Because, Since, As, The reason is..., This occurs because...
C. Effect / Result
Therefore, Consequently, As a result, Thus, Thereby
D. Exemplification
For instance, For example, To illustrate, Namely
E. Contrast
Horizontal, On the other hand, Conversely, In contrast, Nonetheless
F. Clarification
In other words, That is to say, Meaning, Put simply
G. Sequencing
Firstly, Secondly, Next, Subsequently, Lastly
H. Concluding
In conclusion, Ultimately, To sum up, In a nutshell
🚨 Repetitive Filler Alerts
Avoid repeating the same structural transition sequentially (e.g., because → because):
because ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ so ⭐⭐⭐⭐ actually ⭐⭐⭐⭐ more ⭐⭐⭐⭐ well ⭐⭐⭐ maybe like when thing that and also
💡 Concrete Application Examples
Example 1: Workplace Efficiency
Mental Framework: Office is better (J) → High focus (A1) → Accountable environment (A2) → Conclusion (K)
"In my opinion, working from the office is far more effective. The primary reason is that a dedicated workspace drastically improves focus. Furthermore, employees tend to feel a higher level of responsibility due to the physical presence of peers and managers. Consequently, I prefer on-site work over working from home."
Example 2: Managing Fatigue
Mental Framework: Watch anime (J) → Unwinds mind (A1) → Entertainment & insights (A2) → Conclusion (K)
"Whenever I feel burnt out, I usually choose to watch anime. This happens because engaging visuals instantly relax my mind. In addition, I often gain valuable life lessons and sheer entertainment from the storyline. Put simply, anime is my go-to strategy to eliminate stress."
